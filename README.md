# CaliXCEL-Sales-and-Inventory-Management-system-
CaliXCEL (Glass &amp; Aluminum Fabrication Management System) is a system that manages the day-to-day operations of a glass and aluminum fabrication business. It integrates customer records, quotations, projects, inventory, sales contracts, billing, payments, reports, and user administration into a single system.

This system is intended for authorized business staff, including administrators and employees responsible for customer services, quotation preparation and review, proper coordination, inventory, and financial operations. Each user’s access is controlled by their assigned role and permissions. 

The system addresses the difficulty of managing business records and workflows across separate files or manual processes. It helps staff prepare and track quotations, convert approved and signed quotations into projects, monitor project status and material movements, create invoices using the business’s payment milestones, record customer payments, and review operational or financial information according to the permissions. By linking these records, CALIX helps staff follow each job from the initial customer quotation through project work and payment collection.
